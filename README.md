# tab tax (thanks claude)

every tab past ten makes all your pages a little greyer, until you close some.

each extra tab adds 10% grey; at twenty you're in black and white. the icon shows how many you're over, turns it off, or changes the limit. nothing leaves your browser.

get it: https://tadahk.github.io/tab-tax/

build: `node build.js` · tests: `node tests/test.js`
