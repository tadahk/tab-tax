tab tax

every tab past ten makes all your pages a little greyer, until you close some.

each extra tab adds 10% grey. at twenty you're in black and white. the icon shows how many you're over, turns it off, or changes the limit.

download for chrome

install

  1. unzip tab-tax-chrome.zip
  2. open chrome://extensions (chrome, brave, edge, arc, dia, vivaldi)
  3. turn on developer mode, top right
  4. click load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder

chrome web store and firefox add-ons listings are in review.
